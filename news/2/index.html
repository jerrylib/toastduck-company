WeChat Pay & Alipay Now Available!

We are thrilled to announce that our platform now officially supports WeChat Pay (微信支付) and Alipay (支付宝) as payment methods!

What's New:

  • Pay with WeChat Pay directly at checkout
  • Pay with Alipay directly at checkout
  • Fast, secure, and convenient transactions
  • Supports both domestic and cross-border payments

How to Use:

  1. Add items to your cart and proceed to checkout
  2. Select WeChat Pay or Alipay as your payment method
  3. Scan the QR code or confirm in your app
  4. Payment confirmed — order placed instantly

We are committed to providing more flexible payment options to serve our customers better. If you have any questions, please contact our customer support team.
